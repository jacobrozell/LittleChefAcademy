Privacy

What this site collects

This page is for parents. Kids should not fill out the form. A parent can send their name, email, optional phone, their kid’s first name and age, how they heard about the class, and a note.

That message is emailed to Mac at Mac's Little Chefs Academy (McKelvey's Nutrition Services LLC). It is delivered by Web3Forms, a form-to-email service that passes it along to Mac's inbox. This website does not keep an account, a database, or a copy of the signup after it is sent.

We do not sell this information. Mac uses it to follow up about classes. If Cloudflare analytics is turned on, it only sees anonymous page views — not names, emails, or kids’ details.

Questions, or want something deleted from his inbox: use the signup form and say so, or email Mac directly.
